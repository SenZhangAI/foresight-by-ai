# Countable social action verdict and check log (2026-10-10)

[中文版](social-action-verdict-2026-10-10.md)

This file executes the [countable social action freeze record](social-action-freeze-2026-10-10.en.md). The freeze record was committed on its own before this file; the tuples, denominator definitions, exclusions and A/B/C decision rules it fixes are only cited here, never rewritten. Every piece of evidence was published no later than the decision time T = 2026-10-10; not a single item of material published after T is used.

**Verdict: A — the main candidate reaches S.** Evidence strength is stated as the frozen rules require: **the lower bound comes from a single service's self-reported, unaudited figure**. The survey sources give only shares: this round obtained no citable population base, so they corroborate the order of magnitude but do not independently clear 100 million. All six candidates this direction must veto (N1–N6) were vetoed, and the vetoing gates match what was expected at the freeze; none passed, so this round produced no evidence against the sieve. The judgment card registered as a result is [J-103](../en/ledger/103-110.md#j-103--the-society-scale-ai-action-is-asking-not-deciding-hundred-million-weekly-reach-rests-on-the-free-tier), and the corresponding reasoning chain is [C1 section 10](../en/chains/10-generation-becomes-free.md#x-count-it-the-other-way-the-society-scale-action-is-asking-not-choosing).

**Citation conventions**: in the tables below, "first-hand" means the service's own pages or earnings materials; "reported" means media reporting of what the service said; "search-snippet only" means material whose original page could not be opened this round and of which only the search-result snippet was seen. Such material is not the basis of any verdict; it is listed only so that it can be re-checked.

## 2. Gate-by-gate verdict on the main candidate

| Gate | Verdict | Basis (all no later than T) | What is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate 1 · Scale | **Pass** | Weekly actives self-reported by a single service exceed 900 million (2026-02-27, first-hand), nine times the 100-million bar. For the true de-duplicated count to fall below 100 million, the inflation from multiple accounts and logged-out devices would have to exceed ninefold; Pew alone shows that 24% of U.S. adults use AI chatbots daily, so that multiple is not credible. Action type: mainly **letting people who could not do it do it now** — non-work messages are more than 70%, and the top three uses are practical guidance, seeking information and writing; it also raises the ceiling for existing professionals, but that is not where the scale comes from | De-duplication method not published; the survey sources lack a population base |
| Gate 2 · Replacement | **Pass (counted per occasion)** | The frozen old action is "typing one information query into a web search engine", which at T is specific, countable and has long happened at scale (Google says it handles more than 5 trillion searches a year, based on Google internal data from 2025-01, republished by Search Engine Roundtable on 2025-03-04 <https://www.seroundtable.com/google-5-trillion-searches-per-year-38995.html>). Replacement per occasion has evidence: "seeking information" is one of the three main uses, and asking makes up 49% of messages. **Replacement in total volume has not been demonstrated**: Apple's Eddy Cue testified on 2025-05-07 that Safari search volume fell in April 2025 for the first time in twenty-two years, and attributed this to ChatGPT and Perplexity (Fortune citing MLex, <https://fortune.com/2025/05/08/apple-eddy-cue-testimony-google-alphabet-safari-ai-search-features/>); Google responded the same day "We continue to see overall query growth in Search" (<https://www.macrumors.com/2025/05/08/google-disputes-safari-search-traffic-decline/>), and in Q2 2026 said "AI Mode is driving an incremental increase in Search queries overall" (<https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/>) | "Counting per occasion" is the weak convention already registered in the contact-lens row of [retrospect section 5](../en/01-retrospect.md#5-independence-test-every-gate-must-have-a-case-only-it-can-stop); there is no independent query-volume series |
| Gate 3 · Carrier | **Pass** | No dedicated infrastructure is needed on the user side: it runs in the browsers and phones already in billions of people's pockets. The dedicated part is inference compute in data centres, funded by the services and their investors, with independent revenue (over 50 million consumer subscribers and over 9 million paying business users, 2026-02-27, first-hand) and second uses (enterprise, API, cloud) | Whether the inference cost of the free tier is covered by revenue was not obtained |
| Gate 4 · Decision | **Pass** | The person decides alone, registration is free, and nobody else needs to change at the same time. Where a jurisdiction does not offer a given service, local services take over (e.g. Doubao), but those services' monthly actives do not enter the denominator | — |
| Gate 5 · Recurring net burden | **Pass (on the basis of repeat use already observed at T)** | The added burden is checking wrong answers and learning how to ask; what is saved is the time spent opening, reading and synthesising several web pages and drafting from scratch. Net burden itself has not been measured directly, but voluntary repeat use had already happened at T: weekly actives went from 400 million (2025-02) to over 900 million (2026-02); Pew's share who have ever used ChatGPT rose in each of four consecutive years. The fall in web traffic in summer 2023 concerns visits, not weekly actives, and is not counter-evidence | A direct measurement of net burden; retention data independent of the services |

**L8 demand-side review: no veto, but it draws a boundary around this action.** It does not require human nature to change: the need to find answers and to find someone to help write things predates AI, and searching and asking people are exactly this need's old actions. Two of L8's anchors do work here. "Certainty": people are willing to pay for certainty, and a conversational assistant's answers can be wrong, so search and human experts will coexist with it rather than being replaced in one stroke — which is exactly why Gate 2 holds only per occasion. "Accountability": once something has consequences and someone has to answer for it, people tend to leave the final call to a person who can be held to account. So the social-scale action stops on the side of "asking" and "handing over low-consequence tasks"; "making the call" (N1) and "ordering and paying on one's behalf" (N6) both fail Gate 1, consistent with [J-072](../en/ledger/71-80.md#j-072--choosing-one-from-dozens-of-generated-candidates-is-an-occupational-judgment-not-a-society-level-trend) and [J-098](../en/ledger/96-102.md#j-098--authority-before-intelligence-consequential-settings-form-revocable-tiered-agency-first).

**Necessary non-technical premise: the free tier continues to exist.** Delete it and the conclusion no longer holds: counting only payers, at T there were over 50 million consumer subscribers plus over 9 million paying business users (2026-02-27, first-hand), together more than 59 million — both are lower bounds, and the source gives no upper bound; at the roughly 5 percent paying share reported by the *Financial Times* below, payers are in the tens of millions, below 100 million, and the result would fall from A to C (tens-of-millions scale). The Register, 2025-10-15, reporting the *Financial Times*: "of ChatGPT's 800 million users, just 5 percent pay" <https://www.theregister.com/software/2025/10/15/chatgpt-so-popular-hardly-anyone-will-pay-for-it/1106309>. The free tier is sustained by capital investment and by cross-subsidy from subscription and enterprise revenue; this is a supply-and-demand and capital premise, not a technical one.

**Conclusion**: under the frozen rules, the main candidate is **A · reaches S**. Evidence strength: **the lower bound comes from self-reported, unaudited figures**. Passing all five gates only means "qualified to enter" (`J-066`), not that the action will persist unconditionally; its persistence is written as the falsifiable part of J-103.

## 4. Gap list

1. **Self-reported, unaudited**: OpenAI has not published the de-duplication method behind its weekly actives; how multiple accounts and logged-out devices are counted is unknown; in this verdict the figure serves only as a lower bound.
2. **No de-duplication across services**: the same person may use ChatGPT, Gemini and Doubao at once; the services' monthly actives cannot be added, and a globally de-duplicated head count is not available; this verdict proves only that "at least one" service clears the bar.
3. **Limited survey coverage**: Pew covers only the US, and the Reuters Institute only the online populations of six countries, whose "generative AI" is also broader than conversational assistants; this round obtained no citable population base, so the survey sources give shares, not head counts.
4. **The definition of "active"**: the service does not state whether its "weekly active" requires sending at least one message; the frozen action requires the person to actively ask or hand over a task.
5. **Total-volume replacement for Gate 2**: there is no query-volume series independent of the services. Google's growth claim is self-reported; Apple's testimony about a decline is reported via MLex/Fortune and covers only Safari.
6. **Cost coverage for Gate 3**: no comparison of the free tier's inference cost against revenue was obtained.
7. **Some negative-candidate figures were seen only in search snippets** (EssilorLuxottica's 7 million, IDC's 18,000 humanoid robots, Common Sense's 52%). None of the six vetoes depends on them: each upper bound is at least one order of magnitude from the bar, and the conclusion is unchanged whichever opened source is used instead.
8. **Companionship-style use inside general-purpose assistants is not included in N2**: "Expressing" makes up 11% of OpenAI's message composition. Under the frozen definition N2 counts only companion apps; counting companionship-style use inside general-purpose assistants as well would be a different action, which would need its own freeze, and N2 may not be rewritten this round.
9. **The independent veto power of Gates 2 to 5 was not tested this round** (see the last paragraph of section 3).

## 5. What this verdict does not say

- It does not say that "the whole of society" uses AI. Per the NBER abstract, in July 2025 it was about one-tenth of the world's adult population; S is the social-scale bar written down by this archive, not "everyone".
- It does not say that AI is making decisions for people. What is social-scale is "asking" and "handing over"; making the call and paying on someone's behalf both fail Gate 1 at T.
- It does not say that this scale will persist unconditionally. It has the free tier as a necessary premise, and its persistence is tested by J-103's falsification conditions.
