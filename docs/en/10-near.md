# Near-term landscape: 2026–2028

> This is a time-oriented reading guide; the main narrative remains [`chains/10-generation-becomes-free.md`](chains/10-generation-becomes-free.md). The time layer is not a second judgment system: every `J-NNN` links to the single ledger. The near-term change is not simply whether AI can “think,” but how many times equivalent capability can be called.

## 1. Compute and tokens: one answer becomes a batch of attempts

As unit reasoning cost falls, systems will generate, compare, and revise in parallel rather than return one answer. [`J-001`](90-ledger.md#j-001) is the load-bearing judgment; it is **consistent with common expectations, but external comparison is not complete**.

- **More abundant:** equivalent reasoning calls, candidate plans, and automated test runs.
- **More scarce:** worthwhile task boundaries, private context, and judgment before irreversible action.
- **Third question:** objective selection will be automated by the same force and is a window; private context and irreversible action face ownership/privacy, physical, and legal/liability constraints.
- **Who should change behavior:** treat “generate more” as “generate—compare—verify,” while preserving isolation and rollback for real-world actions.

The sequence judgments [`J-006`](90-ledger.md#j-006) and [`J-004`](90-ledger.md#j-004) capture this shift. The strongest opposing mechanism is energy, chip supply, or service queues preventing falling costs from becoming parallel attempts.

## 2. Information and content: production overflows, sources become inputs

Code, images, video, and copy will become easier to synthesize. [`J-002`](90-ledger.md#j-002) tests an important inversion: objective quality selection will mostly be built into generation rather than remain a permanent independent market.

- **More abundant:** format-correct, stylistically similar, reproducible content.
- **More scarce:** unrecorded observation, explicit context, and accountable sources.
- **Third question:** models can remove formal errors, but recombination cannot create a new field observation; physical presence, ownership, and liability are hard constraints.
- **Who should change behavior:** publishers should put source, time, observation conditions, and uncertainty next to the output.

[`J-005`](90-ledger.md#j-005) is the related medium-horizon judgment. Its strongest opposing mechanism is high-fidelity simulation becoming widely accepted as a substitute for observation.

## 3. Attention and trust: being seen is not being believed

When everyone can receive personalized content, attention becomes a question of who deserves time. [`J-017`](90-ledger.md#j-017) provides the structural relationship judgment; its direction is **not yet externally compared**.

- **More abundant:** personalized messages, recommendations, and apparently authentic interaction.
- **More scarce:** consistent identity, accountable commitments, and long relationships.
- **Third question:** a generator can copy surface signals but cannot create years of repeated exchange in one step; trust/relationship and liability are hard constraints.
- **Who should change behavior:** organizations should track fulfilled commitments rather than exposure alone; people should move important judgments from “who sounds right” toward “who bears the consequences.”

The strongest opposing mechanism is a platform internalizing reliable identity and liability so completely that no new credential layer is needed.

## 4. Organizations and employment: fewer people make things, more people coordinate

AI first lowers the cost of documents, scheduling, translation, and plans, then moves the bottleneck to authorization, review, and responsibility. [`J-009`](90-ledger.md#j-009) and [`J-013`](90-ledger.md#j-013) are the relevant technical prerequisites.

- **More abundant:** automatable knowledge-work steps.
- **More scarce:** people who define goals, allocate permissions, and answer for outcomes.
- **Third question:** workflow orchestration can be automated; legal responsibility and organizational trust cannot be borne by generated text.
- **Who should change behavior:** rewrite roles from “produce documents” toward “define boundaries, inspect evidence, and own decisions.”

The strongest opposing mechanism is law assigning responsibility uniformly to platforms, removing the need to redesign organizational boundaries.

## 5. Capital and power: compute gets cheaper, control points do not

Lower reasoning cost does not distribute gains evenly. [`J-001`](90-ledger.md#j-001) supplies the diffusion premise; [`J-005`](90-ledger.md#j-005) points to non-recombinable inputs.

- **More abundant:** compute and generation that small organizations can rent.
- **More scarce:** stable energy, proprietary data, user access, and balance sheets able to absorb losses.
- **Third question:** calls to compute can be copied; energy, ownership, liability, and distribution relationships do not copy at the same speed.
- **Who should change behavior:** evaluate AI projects by who controls inputs, exits, and accident losses, not only by model capability.

This remains a medium-confidence structural reading; the strongest opposing mechanism is complete commoditization of models, energy, and distribution.

## 6. Human needs: more choices do not automatically create new desires

[`J-017`](90-ledger.md#j-017) and the L9 lens set a conservative boundary around demand: contact can multiply while presence and shared consequences remain scarce.

- **More abundant:** identities, works, and life plans to try.
- **More scarce:** relationships willing to share consequences, real experiences, and witnessed commitments.
- **Third question:** surface expression can be generated; shared experience, embodied presence, and repeated trust face physical, relational, and bodily constraints.
- **Who should change behavior:** do not confuse more options with more meaning; reserve time for irreplaceable presence and commitment.

The strongest opposing mechanism is a genuine generational shift in preferences about relationship, status, and presence. If longitudinal behavior shows those anchors drifting, reduce confidence.

## Boundary of this layer

Biology and medicine, education, energy, geopolitics and institutions, and intimate human–AI relationships remain uncovered; see [`90-ledger.md#六未覆盖维度缺口清单`](90-ledger.md#六未覆盖维度缺口清单). This guide does not turn a window into a structural opportunity or turn a low-confidence landscape into a fact.
