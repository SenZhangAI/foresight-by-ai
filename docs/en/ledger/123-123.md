# Judgment card J-123

### J-123 · Expanding remote contact will not automatically increase shared bearing (landscape only)

- **Proposed date**: 2026-10-22
- **One-sentence judgment**: By 2035, the reachable range of courtship, friendship, community, and intergenerational relationships may expand, while shared presence, reciprocal commitment, and conflict repair do not automatically increase at the same rate (landscape only).
- **Audience scale**: UNKNOWN (no independent evidence yet covers relationship identity, repeated action, deduplicated denominator, time, frequency, and source together).
- **Diffusion-gate review**: audience scale = billion-scale (people potentially affected by changing relationship carriers), daily/weekly; Gate 1 **PASS** on the scale axis only; because this card is landscape-only and low-confidence, it must not be cited as a society-wide assertion.
- **What can be done now**: Landscape only: do not use contact counts, message counts, or platform activity as substitutes for relationship quality. Researchers or community organisations can track shared activities, responsibility handovers, conflict repair, and exit outcomes for the same members; until action, denominator, and long-run outcome definitions are frozen, this must not become a universal social trend or an opportunity claim.
- **Lens**: L8 (human nature and demand) + L9 (relational asymmetry); demography, housing, migration, and institutions are parallel starting points, not an AI-only engine.
- **Reasoning chain**: housing, work location, and migration re-bundle repeated contact → communication and AI coordination first expand reachability and weak ties → shared bearing remains constrained by embodied presence, time, institutional rights, and exit costs → contact and responsibility split → the scarce part of relationship quality moves toward sustained reciprocity, shared presence, and conflict repair.
- **Dependency impact**: If long-run cross-institution data show that expanding remote contact brings proportionate increases in shared presence, reciprocal commitment, and conflict repair, this card weakens; if agent involvement produces retention and responsibility fulfilment no worse than human interaction, its boundary must be re-examined.
- **Time window**: 2027–2035.
- **Falsifier**: By 2035, across at least three institutional settings and two relationship types, expanding remote contact coincides with increases in shared presence, reciprocal commitment, and conflict repair, without a split in responsibility fulfilment after agent involvement.
- **Leading indicator**: Shared-activity frequency, responsibility-handover rate, conflict-repair time, and post-exit retention in the same relationship samples; annual.
- **Confidence**: Low (landscape only)
- **Why landscape only**: Telephone, low-cost transport, social-platform, and migration cases have not been screened under a common action, denominator, before/after window, and at least two supporting plus two failing cases; no cross-institutional, cross-generational longitudinal series currently observes reachability, shared presence, and responsibility fulfilment in the same relationship sample.
- **depends-on**: —
- **Strongest opposing mechanism**: Communication, platforms, and agent coordination may expand reachability while also stably organising shared activities, responsibility handovers, and conflict repair, improving relationship outcomes together.
- **Against consensus**: not completed this round; this card is not a restatement of a mainstream forecast. It is retained only as a boundary that historical and long-run behavioural data can refute.
- **External comparison source**: not completed this round; sources for C21’s historical 2+2 calibration are not yet registered.
