# Mid-term landscape: 2029–2032

[Back to README / document map](../../README.en.md)

> In July 2031, a company that handles cross-border returns and claims replaced the dashboard on its wall. The old one showed how many steps had been completed automatically that month; the number kept climbing and stopped meaning anything. The new one keeps a single column, headed “Waiting for a person.” That afternoon the column holds six items, and the one at the top has been waiting nine hours.
>
> Two people in the company can clear that column, and one of them is on parental leave. The other is not short of information: every item arrives with three pages of reasoning the system has written, well organized, better phrased than she would put it herself. What she wants is a different field—what actually happened in the world the last time this procedure ran to the end, rather than what the system expected at the time. That field is often empty. For the empty ones the system can reconstruct a perfectly plausible account, which is good enough for ordering the queue; the moment it goes into a compensation claim, the other side does not accept it.
>
> She is not short of authority either. What she is short of is herself: every item she clears has a line in the contract saying who compensates and up to which tier, and her name goes under that line. That is where the nine hours come from—not a slow system, but her unwillingness to turn something she has not understood into a sentence she is answerable for.
>
> That afternoon she clears four and sends two back. There is still a call to make in the evening: one of the brands lost a great deal this time, and the system has already drafted the email, the compensation plan, and three ways to word it, but the call itself she has to make herself.
>
> That is roughly the shape of the mid term: execution is no longer the bottleneck. The bottleneck becomes that one column—which actions may complete on their own, who decides once one crosses the boundary, and who pays when that decision is wrong. The six dimensions below take that apart. The mid-term is not a jump into science fiction; it carries the written near-term technical judgments into organizational, institutional, and real-world consequences. Every mid-term inference depends on a near-term judgment; if an upstream judgment is falsified, the downstream claim must be reviewed. Full cards live in [`90-ledger.md`](90-ledger.md), and every numbered reference links directly to the ledger, which is the sole source for card fields and status.
>
> This layer spreads one time window out horizontally. To follow a single causal line vertically instead, read [C2 · When Data Is No Longer Free: How Real-World Signals Become Contract Assets](chains/20-real-signals-become-contracts.md)—its argument sits in exactly these years.

## 1. Compute and tokens: generation stops being the bottleneck; execution boundaries become the bottleneck

If [J-001](90-ledger.md#j-001--unit-reasoning-cost-keeps-falling) and [J-006](90-ledger.md#j-006--reasoning-throughput-precedes-long-horizon-autonomy) hold, by around 2029 the same budget can search many candidate plans. The mid-term question is no longer whether a system can generate, but whether it can act continuously without transferring trial-and-error costs to the real world.

- **More abundant:** candidate plans, parallel reasoning, and automatic revision.
- **More scarce:** inspectable permissions, replayable state, and recoverable action.
- **Third question:** simple checks will be internalized; cross-system state, physical harm, and liability face physical and legal/liability constraints.
- **Who should change behavior:** organizations should buy observable, pausable, rollback-capable action environments before buying longer autonomous permissions.
- **Window opportunity:** pausable, replayable, rollback-capable action environments.

[J-010](90-ledger.md#j-010--long-horizon-autonomy-follows-constrained-continuous-execution) depends on [J-009](90-ledger.md#j-009--continuous-execution-in-constrained-workflows-matures-first): if constrained workflows do not stabilize first, both the time window and reliability judgment for long-horizon autonomy weaken. This yields two mid-term judgments: pausable, replayable, rollback-capable action environments become prerequisites ([J-031](90-ledger.md#j-031--pausable-replayable-rollback-capable-action-environments-become-admission-conditions-for-long-horizon-ai-execution)), while authorization review becomes scarcer than execution steps ([J-032](90-ledger.md#j-032--authorization-review-and-exception-escalation-become-scarcer-than-execution-steps)). If [J-010](90-ledger.md#j-010--long-horizon-autonomy-follows-constrained-continuous-execution) is falsified, [J-031](90-ledger.md#j-031--pausable-replayable-rollback-capable-action-environments-become-admission-conditions-for-long-horizon-ai-execution)’s boundary for long-horizon action moves later, while [J-032](90-ledger.md#j-032--authorization-review-and-exception-escalation-become-scarcer-than-execution-steps) remains only inside constrained workflows. The strongest opposing mechanism is a vendor hiding long tasks inside many invisible short workflows and thereby bypassing explicit environment construction.

## 2. Information and content: content becomes feedstock; causality becomes the product

Near-term [J-002](90-ledger.md#j-002--selecting-objectively-high-quality-from-abundant-output-is-not-a-durable-scarcity-but-merely-a-24-year-window) says objective selection will be internalized, while [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) moves value toward raw signals, accountable commitments, and verified causality. The uncomfortable mid-term shift is that explanations become cheap while changing reality remains expensive.

- **More abundant:** explanations, summaries, forecasts, and simulated plans for existing material.
- **More scarce:** new observations, real interventions, and entities willing to bear the cost of being wrong.
- **Third question:** synthetic material can scale automatically, but it cannot automatically complete a real experiment and a legally accepted commitment; physics, time, and liability are hard constraints.
- **Who should change behavior:** researchers and decision-makers should keep “what the model said” separate from “what reality did,” never substituting an elegant explanation for intervention evidence.
- **Window opportunity:** tooling that separates real-intervention records from synthetic evidence.

[J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) branches into two mid-term judgments: verifiable records of real interventions become more valuable than explanation itself ([J-033](90-ledger.md#j-033--verifiable-records-of-real-interventions-become-more-valuable-than-explanation-itself)), while synthetic evidence is accepted first in low-liability contexts and real trials remain required in high-liability contexts ([J-034](90-ledger.md#j-034--synthetic-evidence-is-accepted-first-in-low-liability-contexts-high-liability-contexts-still-require-real-trials)). Both depend on [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality); if [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) is falsified, field signals lose structural premium and both [J-033](90-ledger.md#j-033--verifiable-records-of-real-interventions-become-more-valuable-than-explanation-itself) and [J-034](90-ledger.md#j-034--synthetic-evidence-is-accepted-first-in-low-liability-contexts-high-liability-contexts-still-require-real-trials) weaken. The external comparison is complete: the direction agrees with the common claim that data becomes more important, but the comparison supports only a narrower range — compliance evidence, model-risk governance, and records of real interventions gain value in high-liability settings (EXT-7, EXT-13, EXT-9), with neither superiority to explanation itself nor a time window established. The project therefore retains the more specific claim: unrecorded signals and compensable commitments, not data volume (per-judgment comparison verdicts are in the "Against consensus" fields of [J-033](90-ledger.md#j-033--verifiable-records-of-real-interventions-become-more-valuable-than-explanation-itself) and [J-034](90-ledger.md#j-034--synthetic-evidence-is-accepted-first-in-low-liability-contexts-high-liability-contexts-still-require-real-trials)).

The strongest opposing mechanism is a high-fidelity world model combined with institutions that accept synthetic experiments, making real trials unnecessary. If that occurs, [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) must be reviewed.

See [Historical retrospective · Gate 1](01-retrospect.md#7-running-these-gates-on-what-we-ourselves-have-written) for the criterion and the [ledger review log](90-ledger.md#8-review-log) for card-level evidence.

## 3. Attention and trust: credibility moves from expression to collateral

Near-term [J-017](90-ledger.md#j-017--ai-mediation-expands-weak-tie-coordination-faster-than-strong-relationships) separates weak-tie coordination from strong-tie commitment; [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) says accountable entities themselves will appreciate. If both hold, the more real content looks, the more people ask who pays when it is wrong.

- **More abundant:** personalized explanations, identity narratives, and simulated intimacy.
- **More scarce:** consistency over time, accountability, and trust willing to put assets at risk.
- **Third question:** faces, language, and instant patience can be copied; repeated exchange, relationship repair, and legal responsibility cannot be generated in one step.
- **Who should change behavior:** organizations should build liability, audit, and compensation paths around consequential AI output; individuals should treat credibility as accountability, not natural-sounding tone.
- **Window opportunity:** fulfillment records, responsibility collateral, and audit interfaces.

This produces two mid-term judgments: responsibility collateral enters the transaction structure around consequential AI output ([J-035](90-ledger.md#j-035--responsibility-collateral-enters-the-transaction-structure-for-consequential-ai-output)), and long fulfillment records allocate attention better than one-off natural expression ([J-036](90-ledger.md#j-036--long-term-fulfillment-records-allocate-attention-better-than-one-off-natural-expression)). If [J-017](90-ledger.md#j-017--ai-mediation-expands-weak-tie-coordination-faster-than-strong-relationships) is falsified, the pressure for [J-036](90-ledger.md#j-036--long-term-fulfillment-records-allocate-attention-better-than-one-off-natural-expression)’s credential upgrade weakens because weak-tie coordination did not become cheap; if [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) is falsified, [J-035](90-ledger.md#j-035--responsibility-collateral-enters-the-transaction-structure-for-consequential-ai-output) loses its compensation basis. The strongest opposing mechanism is platforms shifting all responsibility to users while society accepts cheap, low-liability services; then responsibility collateral is a window rather than a structural opportunity.

## 4. Organizations and employment: firm boundaries are set by responsibility, not production capacity

Near-term [J-009](90-ledger.md#j-009--continuous-execution-in-constrained-workflows-matures-first) says constrained workflows mature first, and [J-013](90-ledger.md#j-013--checkable-tool-calls-precede-open-environment-action) says inspectable tool calls spread first. The mid-term result may not be “no people,” but a smaller number of people managing more verifiable agent processes.

- **More abundant:** decomposable, monitorable work steps and the output capacity of small teams.
- **More scarce:** organizational positions that authorize across teams, escalate exceptions, and own final responsibility.
- **Third question:** workflow coordination can be automated; responsibility, relationships, and negotiation after failure remain constrained by law and trust.
- **Who should change behavior:** hiring and education should shift from “can you perform the step?” toward “can you define boundaries, judge exceptions, and bear consequences?”
- **Window opportunity:** permission and exception-escalation layers for small-team agent workflows.

This yields two mid-term judgments: comparable small teams produce more verifiable output ([J-037](90-ledger.md#j-037--comparable-small-teams-produce-more-verifiable-output)), while authorization, exception escalation, and responsibility roles do not shrink at the same rate ([J-038](90-ledger.md#j-038--authorization-exception-escalation-and-responsibility-roles-do-not-shrink-as-fast-as-execution-steps)). If [J-009](90-ledger.md#j-009--continuous-execution-in-constrained-workflows-matures-first) is falsified, [J-037](90-ledger.md#j-037--comparable-small-teams-produce-more-verifiable-output) loses its continuous-execution premise; if [J-013](90-ledger.md#j-013--checkable-tool-calls-precede-open-environment-action) is falsified, [J-038](90-ledger.md#j-038--authorization-exception-escalation-and-responsibility-roles-do-not-shrink-as-fast-as-execution-steps) loses its programmable-permission premise. The strongest opposing mechanism is firms keeping every agent workflow inside vendors and purchasing only outcomes, hiding rather than eliminating boundary change.

## 5. Capital and power: scarcity moves from models to access

If [J-001](90-ledger.md#j-001--unit-reasoning-cost-keeps-falling)’s cost decline and [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality)’s appreciation of real inputs both hold, capital will not only chase the strongest model. It will chase control of energy, data, distribution, licensing, and the ability to compensate for loss.

- **More abundant:** rentable general-purpose reasoning and generation.
- **More scarce:** low-cost energy, exclusive real-world data, critical channels, and balance sheets able to absorb accidents.
- **Third question:** software calls can be copied; ownership, energy access, relationship networks, and liability capacity do not copy at the same speed.
- **Who should change behavior:** founders should value model capability separately from access capability; regulators should watch who privatizes gains and socializes losses.
- **Window opportunity:** access layers for energy, data, channels, and accident solvency.

This yields two mid-term judgments: rented models become abundant while energy, data, and channel control create access rents ([J-039](90-ledger.md#j-039--rented-models-become-abundant-while-energy-data-and-channel-control-create-access-rents)), and balance sheets able to absorb accidents become a separate scarcity ([J-040](90-ledger.md#j-040--balance-sheets-able-to-absorb-ai-accidents-become-a-separate-scarcity)). If [J-001](90-ledger.md#j-001--unit-reasoning-cost-keeps-falling) is falsified, [J-039](90-ledger.md#j-039--rented-models-become-abundant-while-energy-data-and-channel-control-create-access-rents) loses its model-abundance premise; if [J-005](90-ledger.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality) is falsified, [J-040](90-ledger.md#j-040--balance-sheets-able-to-absorb-ai-accidents-become-a-separate-scarcity)’s liability-absorption premium weakens. This is a medium-confidence structural landscape, not an independent opportunity card. The strongest opposing mechanism is rapid commoditization of compute, energy, and data plus timely institutional dispersal of access rights; if access prices lose structural premiums, reduce confidence.

## 6. Human needs and relationships: AI can mediate coordination, not shared experience

[J-017](90-ledger.md#j-017--ai-mediation-expands-weak-tie-coordination-faster-than-strong-relationships) rests on [J-006](90-ledger.md#j-006--reasoning-throughput-precedes-long-horizon-autonomy) and [J-007](90-ledger.md#j-007--resumable-context-precedes-reliable-long-term-memory): communication and shared context become cheap, while strong-relationship capacity remains constrained by attention, bodies, and repeated responsibility. The most tempting mid-term mistake is treating “someone replies for you” as “someone is present for you.”

- **More abundant:** always-available companion replies, relationship-maintenance reminders, and conflict summaries.
- **More scarce:** unmediated shared experience, genuine reciprocity, and repair that the parties must personally undertake.
- **Third question:** language and reminders can be automated; embodied presence, trust, and shared consequences cannot be copied by the same generation force.
- **Who should change behavior:** families, schools, and organizations should reserve shared presence for decisions that cannot be delegated, using AI for preparation and reflection rather than substituted responsibility.
- **Window opportunity:** weak-tie coordination tools; strong-tie presence remains landscape only, not an opportunity.

This yields two mid-term judgments: AI first expands the coordination radius of weak ties ([J-041](90-ledger.md#j-041--ai-first-expands-the-coordination-radius-of-weak-ties)), while shared experience and embodied presence remain the capacity ceiling for strong ties ([J-042](90-ledger.md#j-042--shared-experience-and-embodied-presence-remain-the-capacity-ceiling-for-strong-ties-landscape-only)). If [J-017](90-ledger.md#j-017--ai-mediation-expands-weak-tie-coordination-faster-than-strong-relationships) is falsified, [J-041](90-ledger.md#j-041--ai-first-expands-the-coordination-radius-of-weak-ties) loses its coordination-supply premise; if [J-011](90-ledger.md#j-011--cross-media-consistency-precedes-long-range-coherence) is falsified, [J-042](90-ledger.md#j-042--shared-experience-and-embodied-presence-remain-the-capacity-ceiling-for-strong-ties-landscape-only)’s premise about richer multimodal expression weakens. This is an Exit-B structural landscape with medium-low confidence, not a business opportunity. The strongest opposing mechanism is people treating persistent AI-mediated interaction as sufficiently reciprocal to replace shared experience; if behavior shows stable substitution, rewrite the judgment.
