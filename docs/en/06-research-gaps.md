# Next Research-Gap Priorities

> This is a research queue projected from the existing coverage matrix, `J-NNN` cards, and evolution record—not a second judgment ledger. It answers what to study next, why, and what would count as evidence; it does not raise any judgment's confidence.

## How to read this page

The README coverage matrix marks several dimensions as “Partially covered”: an entry and judgments exist, but institutional, scale, long-term-outcome, or cross-region evidence remains open. This page ranks those existing gaps using the public rule below. The status, card identifiers, and current boundaries remain authoritative in the [coverage matrix](../../README.en.md#coverage-matrix-current-boundary), the linked judgment cards, and [Judgment Evolution](03-evolution.md).

“High priority” does not mean “the forecast is more likely,” nor is it a moral ranking. It is a research order based on **social leverage, upstream dependency, evidence deficit, and executability**. A study may support, narrow, falsify, or honestly stop at `unverified`; all of those outcomes are more valuable than filling a gap with an adjacent topic.

## Ranking rule

The queue is ordered from high to low by:

1. **Social importance**: whether the gap touches basic care, physical labour, institutional rights, public safety, or large resource allocation;
2. **Upstream dependency leverage**: whether the result constrains several reasoning chains rather than one local story;
3. **Decisiveness of the evidence gap**: whether the missing evidence is precisely a scale denominator, institutional boundary, long-term outcome, liability, or cost-allocation measure that could change the judgment;
4. **Executability**: whether the next action can produce an auditable artifact with a defined time point, source material, observation window, and deliverable.

The first three create research value; the fourth breaks ties in favour of work that can actually be tested. Priority is not determined by novelty, commercial visibility, or current confidence. Lower-priority items remain in the archive; they are simply behind higher-leverage, more testable gaps.

## Shared acceptance for research actions

Whichever item is executed first, it must leave independently reviewable material:

- a defined time point `T`, observation window, action definition, and deduplicated denominator;
- stable locations for primary material, with metric definitions and known outcomes/states;
- at least one result that could narrow, falsify, or downgrade the current cards to `unverified`;
- synchronized Chinese and English updates without creating a second judgment fact source;
- if the material only explains a known outcome, mark it `CALIBRATION` rather than future accuracy or a pseudo-out-of-sample hit rate.

If a study cannot supply these prerequisites together, the honest result is to preserve the gap and explain why it stopped, not to lower the evidence standard to fill this table.
