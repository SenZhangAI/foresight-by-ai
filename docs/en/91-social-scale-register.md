**The 0 is the most important thing on this page, not its failure.** This archive built registration, counting and re-review for everything that *fails* a gate (see [Ledger · review log](90-ledger.md#8-review-log)), and never built the symmetric surface for what *passes* — so a reader sees a net of vetoes and cannot tell rigour from idling. This page is that surface: it states the passing count, states where every candidate is stuck and what it lacks, and names **the countable fact that would have to be observed** for it to clear the gate.

This page adds **no new judgment and changes no existing card's gate verdict**. It only aggregates and navigates; every field is authoritative in the [judgment ledger](90-ledger.md) and in the card text under `ledger/`.

## 1. How this 0 was counted

**Definition of the numerator** (all three must hold):

1. the card's "Diffusion-gate review" field records that **Gate 1 passes**;
2. that pass has **not been revoked in place**;
3. the card is not landscape-only — its confidence is neither `Low` nor `Low (landscape only)`. **What this 0 does not say.** It does not say society-scale trends do not exist, and it does not say the method has been falsified. It says exactly one thing: **this archive currently holds no judgment that meets the society-scale threshold it set for itself.**

## 2. What "clearing Gate 1" means

The threshold is the archive's own, not something this page invented. The per-gate ruling table in [Historical retrospective · Gate 1](01-retrospect.md#gate-1--count-the-people-before-you-look-at-the-technology) states the society-scale threshold as:

> **a billion people at daily frequency, or a hundred million at weekly frequency**

What is compared must be **one well-defined action**, counted de-duplicated, with a citable source and a stated frequency. Two failure shapes are already on the record here: adding up several different actions into one "billion" (farm work, domestic work, lifting, care and construction summed together), and passing off an **affected population** (impact reach) as the **actual repeated actors** (the behavioural denominator).

Two further rules decide why this page can only look the way it does:

- **The five gates are a veto filter, not a sufficiency predictor.** One gate failing forces the audience to be narrowed; all five passing only means the claim is still in the running (see [Methodology · The diffusion gates are not a tenth optional lens](00-method.md#the-diffusion-gates-are-not-a-tenth-optional-lens), and card `J-066`). So this page will never say "this one will certainly diffuse".
- **Failing Gate 1 ≠ the judgment is wrong.** [The retrospective](01-retrospect.md#gate-1--count-the-people-before-you-look-at-the-technology) makes the point with professional video-editing software: it fails Gate 1 — its ceiling is the number of editors — yet its penetration among editors approaches 100% and it is a good business. The correct reading of a Gate 1 failure is: **the ceiling equals the size of that group, and it will not become a society-wide norm.**

## 3. Answering one question verbatim: where are all these people who need AI to decide for them?

This project received the following challenge; it is quoted verbatim (the original is Chinese, with a translation below):

> 我完全没想到有一个通用的，能成为整个社会风气的趋势，手机能成为趋势，在线直播能成为趋势，AI 哪有那么多要决策的人？

> "I simply cannot see one general thing here that could become a society-wide norm. The mobile phone became one. Live streaming became one. Where are all these people who have so much to decide?"

**Answer: by the archive's own threshold, it really is 0 right now. The challenge holds, and it has already rewritten this archive.**

What it identified was concrete. Chain C1 opens with a marketing lead who receives sixty finished proposals and spends two full afternoons deciding nothing, and from there derives "choosing and trading off becomes scarce". [Section 7 of the retrospective](01-retrospect.md#7-running-these-gates-on-what-we-ourselves-have-written) ran that claim through a mechanical head count: with the action defined as "faced with a batch of generated candidates, select one and own the outcome", the people who simultaneously (a) produce candidates in batches as a job, (b) hold the decision rather than execute it, and (c) do so at least weekly are concentrated in the decision layer of a few occupations — globally **million-scale, weekly**, which is two to three orders of magnitude below a billion daily or a hundred million weekly. It was registered as `J-072`, an occupational judgment that may not be written in the voice of "the whole of society". The 2026-09-20 review then applied the same ruler to the whole ledger, which is where the 98 in section 1 comes from.

**"What observation would make it no longer 0"** is the most useful thing this page can give, and it is not a criterion invented here — the same section already points the way:

> **So what is at society scale?** On the same chain, what clears Gate 1 is not "choosing", it is "making". "Needing a usable document, image, piece of copy or program" — a billion people occasionally need that, and until now most of them **could not do it**. That belongs to the "lets people who could not do it do it" class, and that ceiling is a society-scale one.

Turning that into a countable fact requires four things **at once**; miss one and the count stays 0:

1. **One action, not a class of situations.** The action must fit in a sentence and be countable by a third party (the phone's action is "carry a screen and glance at it at any moment"; live streaming's is "open a stream and watch someone do something in real time").
2. **A de-duplicated actor denominator at the threshold.** A billion de-duplicated individuals daily, or a hundred million de-duplicated individuals weekly, performing **the same** action above. People — not devices, not accounts, not call volume, and not "affected population".
3. **A citable source and an observation window.** The denominator comes from citable statistics or a recomputable measurement, stating the metric definition, the point in time and the frequency — not a constructed estimate.
4. **Gates 2 to 5 do not veto.** It substituted for a real prior action (Gate 2), its carrier was built for other reasons anyway (Gate 3), adoption does not require others to change at the same time (Gate 4), and the recurring net burden relative to the real incumbent is not positive and substantial (Gate 5).

This page deliberately does **not** name "the one most likely to clear it first". Until historical calibration and the isolated re-review are closed out, this project does not open new prediction work, and naming a front-runner would be exactly that: one more uncalibrated judgment. Section 4 gives each candidate its own countable fact; which one is observed first is settled by observation, not by this page.

## 6. How this 0 stops being 0

The path is ordered; the steps cannot be skipped:

1. **Obtain an adequate denominator first.** Pick a row from Group 3 and measure the countable fact in its last column: one well-defined action, de-duplicated actors, a stated frequency and point in time, a citable source. If it cannot be obtained, write "unknown" honestly rather than passing impact reach off as a denominator — that substitution is exactly where these cards are stuck now.
2. **Then clear Gates 2 to 5.** Clearing Gate 1 only means the scale is there. What it substituted for, who builds the carrier, who must change at the same time, and whether the recurring net burden relative to the real incumbent is positive or negative — all four need answers; a card with no unlock condition may not simply push its year back.
3. **Only then is the society-scale writing permission granted — and it can still be withdrawn.** `J-029` lost it after having it: the permission comes from the criterion, never from the voice.

Running in parallel is what Groups 5 and 6 are owed: **a criterion that can rule against institutional and allocation claims, calibrated first against historical cases** as this project requires (at least 2 successes + 2 failures each, and it must be able to rule "no" on some real case). Without that criterion, those two groups stay at "no criterion", not at "passed".
