# Technology Capability Sequence

> This entry answers what arrives first, what it enables, and what follows. Consequences are developed in the time-layer narratives.

## Initial chain

1. Unit reasoning cost continues to fall (J-TECH-001).
2. Systems generate more candidates across code, images, video, and execution plans.
3. Formalizable quality selection is absorbed by models, making general quality tools a window (J-SCAR-001).
4. Complex workflows hit private context and irreversible action, making ownership and rollback new constraints (J-SCAR-002, J-SCAR-003).
