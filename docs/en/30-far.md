# Far-Term Landscape (about 2033–2041)

> Far-term uncertainty includes human preference and institutional response. Most content here is landscape only until evidence makes it testable.

## Chains to develop

- If generation, judgment, and some execution become highly automated, whether presence, responsibility, and status become costlier credentials.
- How energy, physical space, capital ownership, and social permission constrain diffusion.
- Which needs persist and which change as supply changes.

## Landscape only for now

Do not turn imagination into an opportunity claim. Every new judgment must cite its J-NNN dependencies and pass the question of whether the same force can automate it.

## What can be done now

Build monitoring indicators and opposing scenarios rather than placing an early bet. Only directions that pass the hard-constraint gate belong in the opportunity file.
