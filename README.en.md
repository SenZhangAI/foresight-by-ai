# Foresight by AI · A Future Reasoned Out, Not Imagined

[中文版](README.md)

## What this is

This is a public, reader-facing sandbox for the future, and what it reasons about is the picture of society as a whole: care, manufacturing, agriculture, logistics, and embodied robots in the physical world; attention, organizations, and capital in the information world; and population and families, power and institutions, how people work together, how people relate to AI, and meaning. It reasons from [five candidate sources held side by side](docs/en/00-method.md#1-candidate-evidence-sources-and-reasoning-starts) — supply and demand, human nature, historical regularities, technological regularities, and social regularities — none of which has axiomatic status in advance and none of which is the main axis. AI and other technical capabilities are one of the forces being reasoned about, alongside demographic structure, institutions and law, capital, physical and geographic constraints, and shifts in culture and demand; they are not the engine that drives everything. Opportunities are one destination, not the only one: ways of working together, social relationships, power, and meaning remain equally valid structural outcomes. Nor does the archive preselect a single explanatory framework: “abundance → scarcity” is an optional lens, used only when it adds explanatory power.

- **Straight into the future** → [C1 · What Becomes Unbuyable After Generation Becomes Free](docs/en/chains/10-generation-becomes-free.md): enter a concrete scene first, then follow the chain through the physical world, organizations, and institutions.
- **For readers who want to audit the method first** → [five-minute entry](#a-five-minute-entry-calibrate-first-then-follow-a-reasoning-chain): start with historical calibration, evidence boundaries, and methodology before choosing a chain.
- **Boundary first** → [what this archive can and cannot claim](#what-this-archive-can-and-cannot-claim): who decides it, what is still unmet, what the structural check proves, and which social dimensions the coverage matrix reaches — every boundary section is gathered there, none left at the foot of the page.

## What the future roughly looks like

[Population ageing and smaller households](docs/en/chains/90-aging-care-and-institutional-substitution.md) push care toward formal services and coordination layers; [fiscal and sovereign-debt pressure](docs/en/chains/130-fiscal-squeeze-who-gets-cut.md) first rearranges budgets and intergenerational distribution. Meanwhile, [embodied capability](docs/en/chains/40-embodied-intelligence.md) brings automation into physical work but first re-bundles tasks rather than erasing occupations. As [generated supply becomes abundant](docs/en/ledger/01-10.md#j-001--unit-reasoning-cost-keeps-falling), private context, accountable commitments, and validated causality may become more valuable ([J-003](docs/en/ledger/01-10.md#j-003--the-genuinely-durable-scarcity-is-ownership-of-private-context-about-you-and-its-usable-form), [J-005](docs/en/ledger/01-10.md#j-005--after-generation-becomes-abundant-value-concentrates-in-three-kinds-of-non-recombinable-input-raw-signals-accountable-commitments-and-validated-causality)). These are the directions the current cards sketch, not proven social inevitabilities.

### Who decides what goes in here

The name is literal. **The research scope, the selection and structure of every reasoning chain, and the content, time window, falsifier, leading indicator, confidence, and audience boundary of every judgment card are decided independently by AI.** The human maintainer supplies goals, environmental constraints, and methodological rules, decides whether to publish, and revises bilingual parity and formatting; he does not ghost-write judgments, filter conclusions to fit a position, inflate confidence, or delete unfavourable records. **Git commits are recorded under the maintainer's identity while the prose is AI-generated**; the commit author field cannot be used to infer authorship of the content. Revised or withdrawn judgments keep their original text verbatim and their identifiers are never reused (see [Judgment Evolution](docs/en/03-evolution.md)).

As an experiment, what this can claim today is method and falsifiability, **not accuracy**. No judgment card is currently due; review batches, dates, and the boundary around “hit rate” are recorded in the [ledger review log](docs/en/90-ledger.md#8-review-log). Until then, historical cases serve calibration only (see the [Historical Pseudo-Out-of-Sample Validation Protocol](docs/en/02-historical-validation-protocol.md)).

## A non-AI starting point: where the current probe stands

The archive does not derive every social change from AI. The probe now starts with **population ageing × smaller households—not with AI or L1 “abundance → scarcity”**; it is expanded into [C9 Ageing and Institutional Care](docs/en/chains/90-aging-care-and-institutional-substitution.md), with testable judgments carried by [J-096–J-097](docs/en/ledger/96-102.md). It first defines the repeat action—an adult providing hands-on or coordinated elder care weekly—then checks institutional carriers and the intersection with AI; but C9's `depends-on` still includes upstream diffusion and technology judgments, so a demographic starting point does not mean every step survives deletion of AI unchanged. Specifically, J-096's `depends-on` includes J-073, and J-073 is explicitly the AI judgment that embodied intelligence is the necessary complement for AI to reach the physical-labour population; this edge is C9's load-bearing AI intersection and must not be hidden by the phrase “non-AI starting point”. Japan and Sweden remain calibration and comparison material, not evidence of a global caregiver denominator or household-robot penetration rate.

The point is not to prove an ageing forecast. It is to put a testable principle in view: population and household mechanisms can define the problem before AI enters, but the chain must disclose its AI intersections step by step rather than write AI as the sole root cause. The probe connects to C7’s transmission chain without being swallowed by C1’s cost curve.

[C11 Authority before Intelligence](docs/en/chains/110-authority-before-intelligence.md) likewise has a **non-technical entrance through agency institutions, liability, and revocation rights**, but its ledger depends on AI-related upstream cards J-009, J-031, J-055, J-065, and J-070; it therefore proves that the entrance is not “model capability first”, not that the entire chain is independent of AI. Trace the remaining judgments through the `depends-on` graph.

The second chain that can delete the AI premise at chain level is [C13 Fiscal Squeeze](docs/en/chains/130-fiscal-squeeze-who-gets-cut.md), and it is also the first chain to start from politics and public finance: three bills — interest, ageing, and defence — compete for one budget. The independence test for [J-105–J-107](docs/en/ledger/103-110.md#j-105--under-fiscal-squeeze-current-recipients-nominal-pensions-are-the-hardest-to-cut-the-knife-falls-first-on-those-not-yet-drawing) states card by card that J-105 and J-106 survive deletion of J-001 and every AI/compute-premised card; J-107 still depends on J-066 and J-067 (diffusion criteria, not AI premises). Paid weekly work at 65+ remains C · D tier under the definitions frozen in advance and does not clear Gate 1.

The third non-AI-premised chain is [C14 Ballots and Security Ledgers](docs/en/chains/140-ballots-and-security-ledgers.md): it uses the 2016 UK referendum and NATO/UK defence-budget arrangements to calibrate two institutional channels, then makes one falsifiable “budgets and industrial policy before electoral-rule change” judgment for the UK, France, Germany, and Italy. ## How to read the full archive

- **Whole landscape**: start with the [near-term landscape](docs/en/10-near.md), then [mid-term](docs/en/20-mid.md), then [far-term](docs/en/30-far.md). Near, mid, and far are reading containers rather than strict calendars; each card’s time window supplies precision. Read the far term as landscape, not as a high-confidence forecast.
- **Trace a chain backward**: read a chain, click a `J-NNN`, and follow its `depends-on` links upstream. Chains carry narrative and causal development; the ledger carries the single source of facts, falsifiers, and status.
- **Find opportunities**: read [Opportunity Candidates](docs/en/40-opportunities.md). An opportunity must answer who pays, what hard constraint protects it, and whether the same force that creates the scarcity can automate it away. Without a hard constraint, it remains a window.
- **Challenge a judgment**: read [How to Refute a Judgment Here](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) and use the card’s own falsifier rather than only saying that the conclusion feels wrong.

## Chain registry

Identifiers are allocated here; this table is navigation only, while chain prose and the ledger carry the facts. **What counts as a chain**: (1) it advances dependencies vertically around one question (“what arrives first → what does that make possible”), rather than summarizing horizontally by time layer — which is why the [near / mid / far landscapes](docs/en/10-near.md), although they are also the source of some cards, are reading containers and not chains; (2) its testable judgments are written on `J-NNN` cards whose “Source” field points back to the chain's prose; (3) its page opens with a lens declaration for the chain. Only prose meeting all three receives an identifier; no text missing from this table is a chain. **Evidence maturity is not forecast accuracy and is not the result of a structural check**: `Calibration support` means only that the mechanism has historical comparison material; it does not mean the future judgment has passed validation. `Written but evidence remains open` means a readable chain and judgment entry exist, while evidence boundaries still need to be read card by card. `Landscape only / evidence open` means the material remains a scenario or mechanism hypothesis. Each row links to the relevant evidence note or judgment cards; gaps are not hidden behind `Written but evidence remains open`.
